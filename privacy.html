Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026
In short

Al-Mathurat does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no data ever leaves your device.

  • Everything you set — language, theme, text size, reading progress, reminder times — stays only on your phone.
  • Reminders are scheduled entirely on-device; nothing is sent to us or anyone else to make them work.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data, because none of it exists anywhere else.

What this app is

Al-Mathurat (አል-መዕሡራት) is a daily Islamic remembrance (azkar) app, showing Arabic text alongside Amharic and English pronunciation and meaning. This policy covers the Android and iOS apps published under this name.

Information we collect

None. Al-Mathurat has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server backend of any kind. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services, and it does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other device data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally, using your device's own storage, purely so your preferences and progress persist between sessions:

  • Your chosen interface language, content language, and theme (light, night, or automatic)
  • Text size and display preferences
  • Which morning/evening azkar and which of the two versions (Kubra/Sugra) you have completed today
  • Your reminder times, if you turn reminders on

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It is readable only by the app, on your device, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications directly on your device using your operating system's own notification scheduler. This does not involve a push-notification service or any external server — the reminder is set and delivered entirely on your phone. You can disable this at any time in the app's Settings, or by revoking notification permission in your device's system settings.

Children's privacy

Al-Mathurat is suitable for users of any age. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children, and none of the concerns that a data-collecting app would raise for younger users apply here.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does involve data leaving your device — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly. We will not add such a feature without updating this policy first.
